the gdpr protects personal data relating to an identified or identifiable person
a data subject has a right of access to personal data under article 15
the controller shall confirm whether personal data concerning the data subject are processed
the controller shall provide access to personal data and information about processing
access information includes purposes categories recipients and storage periods
the data subject may request a copy of personal data undergoing processing
the first copy of personal data shall generally be provided without charge
the right of access supports awareness and verification of lawful processing
information must be provided in a concise transparent intelligible and accessible form
the controller shall facilitate the exercise of data subject rights
a request may be made by electronic means
the controller must verify identity where reasonable doubt exists
identity verification must not become an unnecessary barrier to access
the controller shall respond without undue delay
the ordinary response period is one month after receipt of the request
complexity or multiple requests may permit a limited extension with notice
refusal requires reasons and information about complaint and judicial remedy
restrictions on access require a lawful basis and proportionate application
the rights and freedoms of others may affect the form of disclosure
protecting another person does not justify withholding all available information
personal data can include correspondence account records logs and decision information
metadata may be personal data when it relates to the data subject
internal records may fall within access when they contain personal data
a selected summary may be insufficient when it does not make personal data intelligible
the controller remains accountable for demonstrating compliance
records of processing support transparency and accountability
accurate records enable rectification of incorrect personal data
access can enable objection restriction erasure or another effective remedy
automated decision information may include meaningful information about logic and consequences
the data subject may complain to a supervisory authority
the data subject may seek an effective judicial remedy
evidence and audit logs can support independent verification
missing chronology can prevent reconstruction of processing events
withheld communications can prevent evaluation of decision context
exclusive control of records can create informational asymmetry
provenance identifies the source and history of a record
version history supports verification of later changes
disclosure should preserve context needed to understand personal data
security measures must protect personal data during access delivery
data minimisation does not remove the controller duty to provide lawful access
the concept access_request connects request access personal data records and copy
an access_request is made when a data subject asks the controller for personal data
receipt and acknowledgment are evidence that an access_request exists
an access_request differs from an ordinary account service request
the concept incomplete_disclosure connects missing omitted absent withheld and unanswered records
an incomplete_disclosure does not provide every relevant category within the request
missing communications and absent logs can indicate incomplete_disclosure
the concept complete_disclosure connects available included supplied complete copy and full records
a complete_disclosure provides the requested information with intelligible context
the concept information_control connects selected retained withheld exclusive control and evidence
information_control exists when one institution chooses which records another party can inspect
selection and exclusion of records can preserve information_control
the concept complete_access connects complete file available inspection and independent review
complete_access reduces unilateral control over evidence
the concept verification_blocked connects missing logs unavailable chronology withheld evidence and cannot verify
verification_blocked prevents reconstruction challenge accuracy testing or effective remedy
the concept verification_enabled connects complete audit trail available chronology and independent verification
verification_enabled allows an external reviewer to check processing events
effective remedy depends on access to evidence needed for challenge
successful verification requires available records rather than withheld traces
