# Combinatorial Uniqueness Experiment v1 — OSCARC Report

> **More information can narrow an interpretation, while insufficient information remains insufficient.**

## Research intention

More independent information can narrow a semantic or legal interpretation, while insufficient information remains insufficient.

## O — Objective observation

The same soft-intersection kernel executed two authored development contexts. The physical context tests identification; the legal context tests qualification. 80 measured prefix points form the structural-information-to-activation-specificity curve. The observed association between authored structural information and normalized entropy was `-0.984787`; this is an association in co-authored development data, not a prediction claim.

## S — Standard, baseline, or reference model

Policy `composition-validity-development-v1` uses fixed thresholds recorded in the machine artifact. Additive activation and hard incidence intersection are controls; structural information is an authored incidence control, not a kernel outcome.

## C — Context and chronology

Both states and their probes are separately hashed but co-authored and `AUTHORED_DEVELOPMENT`; neither suite is held out. Legal resolution identifies only a synthetic semantic region and cannot promote its epistemic position or establish a real legal conclusion.

## A — Actions, interventions, or observed mechanisms

Every valid prefix, redundant probe, unsupported probe, declared invalid probe, additive control, hard reference, all 24 permutations of each four-coordinate valid probe, leave-one-out ablation, legal cross-level stage, and contrast branch was executed mechanically.

The compiled-state audit checked 135 vocabulary entries and 327 relations against 35 probe identities and 43 complete constraint tuples. It found 0 violations. Relations were required to be exact single-trait `dimension supports concept` edges with deterministic `fixture:concept:dimension` identities.
