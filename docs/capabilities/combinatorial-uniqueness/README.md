# Programme 3: Combinatorial Uniqueness

## Capability Target

> **Specificity created through coordinate composition.**

Combinatorial Uniqueness asks whether several individually broad but sufficiently independent governed semantic coordinates can combine at runtime into a narrow, distinctive conceptual target or semantic region.

In compact form:

```text
broad coordinate A
  + broad coordinate B
  + broad coordinate C
  -> distinctive query-time intersection
```

This is the third MML capability: **Compose concepts.**

## Dependency On The First Two Capabilities

Composition requires:

1. **Semantic Representation:** the primitive coordinates must be explicit, governed, and addressable.
2. **Knowledge State Execution:** their relations, memberships, exclusions, and activation behavior must exist in named compiled state.
3. **Combinatorial Uniqueness:** runtime execution can then test whether their intersection creates useful specificity.

A numerical intersection can be computed even when the representation is wrong or the compiled state is incomplete. That does not make the result semantically valid.

## What The Capability Defines

Combinatorial Uniqueness defines:

- each **single coordinate** as a broad reference field;
- **every pair** needed to expose marginal narrowing and correlated constraints;
- the **full combination** as the candidate distinctive coordinate;
- progressive independent combinations;
- matched redundant combinations at equal coordinate counts;
- unsupported and explicitly contradictory combinations;
- Boolean hard intersection;
- additive activation;
- normalized geometric-mean soft intersection;
- dense-retrieval comparison where a frozen document corpus exists;
- lexical–MML fusion where surface evidence matters;
- coordinate ablation and leave-one-out marginal contribution;
- operational independence controls such as incidence overlap, Jaccard similarity, mutual information, or candidate elimination;
- order and permutation controls;
- governed validity and rejection rules; and
- separation of numerical mechanism resolution from governance resolution.

## Implemented Mechanism Versus Scaling Proposition

The repository implements independent activation fields and normalized geometric-mean soft intersection. That mechanism prevents one strong field from completely dominating candidates weakly supported by another field.

The broader [Combinatorial Scaling Proposition](proposition.md) is not established by implementing soft intersection. It asks whether a reusable basis of governed coordinates can cover a substantially larger useful conceptual space without bespoke primitives for every meaningful combination.

The operational capability also exposes `compose_candidate_region`, a small exact-incidence operator over compiled coordinate postings. Programme 4 consumes it to retrieve complete compatible regions without soft ranking or forced uniqueness. That reuse demonstrates interoperability; exact retrieval parity does not establish this programme's stronger soft-intersection or scaling proposition.

The distinction is:

- **implemented:** independent fields, soft intersection, additive and hard controls, governed validity checks, ablations, two authored development contexts, and one structurally held-out synthetic signature split;
- **not established:** a distinctive MML advantage on held-out combinations, independently authored generalization, useful basis coverage, scaling across a large realized combination space, independence across real domains, and net efficiency after representation and governance cost.

## Research Question

> Does independently composed governed semantic information produce progressively greater specificity than single, redundant, additive, and hard-intersection controls on combinations absent when the knowledge state was compiled?

## Required Treatments

For each target or region, compare:

1. every single coordinate;
2. every required pair;
3. every progressive independent prefix;
4. the complete combination;
5. matched redundant combinations;
6. Boolean hard intersection;
7. additive activation;
8. geometric-mean soft intersection;
9. a dense baseline when a document corpus is present;
10. lexical–MML fusion when surface-language evidence is relevant;
11. leave-one-coordinate-out ablations;
12. all relevant coordinate orders or a balanced order design; and
13. unsupported and declared-invalid cases with distinct rejection reasons.

No node, edge, alias, primitive, or weight may encode a complete evaluation combination as a bespoke shortcut.

## Measurements

Report separately:

- target rank and deterministic tie handling;
- target-versus-runner-up margin;
- plausible candidate count;
- entropy, normalized entropy, concentration, and top-k mass;
- intended-target precision, recall, MRR, and nDCG where judgments support them;
- specificity gain per added coordinate;
- independent-versus-redundant gain at matched coordinate counts;
- hard-negative intrusion and zero-overlap recovery;
- valid resolution, unsupported non-resolution, and contradiction rejection rates;
- coordinate ablation and marginal contribution;
- order sensitivity and final-set invariance;
- threshold and policy sensitivity;
- bespoke-combination leakage checks; and
- representation, compilation, governance, storage, and execution cost.

## Current Evidence

The first combined [Combinatorial Uniqueness Experiment v1](results/v1.md) executed physical identification and synthetic legal qualification through the same soft-intersection kernel. That aggregate is now **`SUPERSEDED_BY_DECOMPOSITION`** and is not the current programme verdict. Its declared access-to-evidence trajectory numerically favored `evidence_asymmetry`, but its governed direct intersection was empty and correctly stayed unresolved; that observation remains part of the evidence record.

That failure exposed three different semantic operations, now tested atomically:

| Experiment | Operation | Development result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [3.1 — Direct Combinatorial Intersection](results/direct-intersection-v1.md) | Cumulative coordinates remain requirements of one target. | `LOCALLY_CONSISTENT`: 8/8 authored targets resolved; redundant controls stayed broad; 3/3 declared-invalid combinations were rejected. |
| [3.2 — Governed Legal Qualification](results/governed-legal-qualification-v1.md) | Direct legal-region qualification with contrast, refusal, and epistemic non-promotion. | `LOCALLY_CONSISTENT`: authored qualifications and contrasts resolved while insufficient combinations remained unresolved. |
| [3.3 — Cross-Level Semantic Transition](results/cross-level-semantic-transition-v1.md) | Each declared stage has local constraints; the previous region becomes antecedent provenance. | `CONSISTENT`: all authored stages resolved while failed flat cumulative conjunction remained visible as the control. |
| [3.4 — Compositional Generalization](results/compositional-generalization-v1.md) | Pairwise-only construction is tested against disjoint complete signatures in a 20-dimensional representation generated from four latent variables. | `EXECUTION_CONFORMANT`: MML executed the held-out projected representation, but scaling claim `NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_THIS_FIXTURE` because exact controls matched MML's curve. |

These results do not turn the original failed conjunction into a success. They show that direct intersection and semantic transition require different contracts. The [cross-level root-cause report](studies/cross-level-root-cause.md) preserves the numerical, representational, and governance diagnosis.

## Evidence Boundary

The physical and legal fixtures are synthetic, co-authored with the mechanism, and not held out. Experiment 3.4 adds a structurally disjoint signature split and demonstrates execution over a 20-dimensional representation generated from four latent variables, but its exact controls match MML and its generator realizes only 256 complete signatures. The programme therefore does not establish natural-language understanding, factual legal adjudication, production-scale performance, independently authored cross-domain generalization, a distinctive soft-intersection advantage, or the full combinatorial scaling proposition. Compiled encyclopaedic retrieval now belongs to the accumulated [Semantic Navigation programme](../semantic-navigation/README.md), which consumes exact candidate-region composition without treating retrieval parity as evidence for this programme's stronger uniqueness claim.
