# Direction Concept Ledger

## Purpose

This subledger reserves the `direction` branch in the first concept tree of the Seed Vault.

It is the concept-governance surface for orientation-oriented semantic identities such as purpose, vector, intent, governance, priority, alignment, and developmental trajectory.

## Subledgers

| Subledger | Current role |
| --- | --- |
| [Direction Core Concept Ledger](Direction-Core-Concept-Ledger.md) | foundational direction concepts |
| [Direction Governance Concept Ledger](Direction-Governance-Concept-Ledger.md) | governance and stewardship concepts |
| [Direction Alignment Concept Ledger](Direction-Alignment-Concept-Ledger.md) | coordination and interoperability concepts |

## Admission Rule

Concepts entering this subledger should primarily name:

- purpose or objective classes;
- orientation or vector concepts;
- governance and prioritization concepts;
- intentionality and alignment concepts; or
- development-path abstractions that are not better modeled as `form` identities or `movement` processes.
