# Direction Alignment Concept Ledger

## Purpose

This subledger records direction concepts used when the project talks about institutional fit, interoperability, and future-oriented coordination.

## Concepts

| Local id | Canonical label | Status | Boundary confidence | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `direction.interoperability` | `interoperability` | `candidate` | medium | SOS initiative alignment | Directed fit that allows systems or vocabularies to work together without collapse |
| `direction.coordination` | `coordination` | `candidate` | medium | Initiative alignment and governance materials | Directed organization across actors, systems, or institutions |
| `direction.federation` | `federation` | `candidate` | medium | Dual-engine direction and SOS materials | Multi-party direction concept in which several governed systems remain distinct but connected |

| `direction.regeneration` | `regeneration` | `candidate` | medium | Seed Vault analogy | Future-oriented recovery or reuse of preserved material |

## Governance Notes

- These concepts are directional because they orient how the system should evolve and connect, not just what it contains.
- `interoperability` and `coordination` should remain separate even when they often co-occur institutionally.
