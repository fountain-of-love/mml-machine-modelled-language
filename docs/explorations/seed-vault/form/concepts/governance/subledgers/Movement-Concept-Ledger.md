# Movement Concept Ledger

## Purpose

This subledger reserves the `movement` branch in the first concept tree of the Seed Vault.

It is the concept-governance surface for movement-oriented semantic identities such as relation, flow, transformation, resonance, coupling, transfer, and processual change.

## Subledgers

| Subledger | Current role |
| --- | --- |
| [Movement Core Concept Ledger](Movement-Core-Concept-Ledger.md) | foundational movement concepts |
| [Movement Observatory Concept Ledger](Movement-Observatory-Concept-Ledger.md) | discovery and observation movement concepts |
| [Movement RCL Concept Ledger](Movement-RCL-Concept-Ledger.md) | RCL-derived movement concepts |

## Admission Rule

Concepts entering this subledger should primarily name:

- relations or relation families;
- transformations or process classes;
- flow and transfer concepts;
- coupling, interaction, or propagation concepts; or
- movement-specific abstractions that are not better modeled as `form` identities or `direction` orientations.
