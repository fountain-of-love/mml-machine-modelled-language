# Direction

## Identity

- Canonical label: `direction`
- Local id: `form.direction`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Direction is the structural orientation by which a relation, process, interpretation, or development is ordered toward one side, end, or vector rather than another.

## Boundary

Included:

- orientation
- vector or ordered tendency
- source-to-target asymmetry
- declared towardness in interpretation or development

Excluded:

- movement itself
- a static location with no orientation
- arbitrary sequence without ordered significance
- governance intent unless explicitly modeled as a directional variant

## Aliases

- orientation
- vector
- towardness
