# Activation

## Identity

- Canonical label: `activation`
- Local id: `form.activation`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Activation is the condition or event through which a latent capacity, stored state, or structured possibility becomes effective, expressed, or operative.

## Boundary

Included:

- enabling event
- transition into effective operation
- realized triggering of latent potential
- condition that makes a structure operative

Excluded:

- stable capacity considered apart from use
- the full downstream flow after activation
- a static classification label
- passive substrate without operative change

## Aliases

- triggering
- enabling condition
- operative activation

## Provenance

- Seed Vault role vocabulary around activation and gain
- Seed Vault structural concept-layer initialization

## Open Questions

- Is activation best modeled as an event, a condition, or a family spanning both?
- How sharply should activation be distinguished from excitation, triggering, and flow onset?
