# Substrate

## Identity

- Canonical label: `substrate`
- Local id: `form.substrate`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Substrate is the bearer, medium, or supporting basis in which a structure, state, or capacity is realized.

## Boundary

Included:

- carrier or medium
- material or formal basis of realization
- supporting layer in which a process or structure exists

Excluded:

- the relation between two concepts
- the activation event that changes state
- a purely external observer description with no bearing role
- a temporary output detached from its bearer

## Aliases

- medium
- carrier
- supporting basis
