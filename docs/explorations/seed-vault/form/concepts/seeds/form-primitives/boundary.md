# Boundary

## Identity

- Canonical label: `boundary`
- Local id: `form.boundary`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Boundary is the structural distinction that determines what lies within, what lies outside, and what may pass, couple, or apply between the two under declared conditions.

## Boundary

Included:

- inside versus outside distinction
- interface or limit condition
- transfer or admission condition
- scope-defining separation

Excluded:

- the flow that crosses a boundary
- the substrate as such
- an arbitrary category split without structural consequence
- a moral or legal rule unless the concept is explicitly recast in that domain

## Aliases

- interface boundary
- system boundary
- boundary condition
