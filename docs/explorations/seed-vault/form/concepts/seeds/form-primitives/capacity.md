# Capacity

## Identity

- Canonical label: `capacity`
- Local id: `form.capacity`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Capacity is the stable ability of a structure, substrate, or system to admit, hold, transmit, or make available some quantity, state, or potential under declared conditions.

## Boundary

Included:

- storage or holding ability
- admissible carrying or transfer ability
- structural allowance or receptivity
- bounded potential for later activation or use

Excluded:

- the realized flow itself
- the momentary activation event
- a governance decision about whether use is permitted
- a measurement outcome that depends on unrelated external causes

- holding capacity
- receptive capacity
