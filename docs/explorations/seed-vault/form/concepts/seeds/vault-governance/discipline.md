# Discipline

## Identity

- Canonical label: `discipline`
- Local id: `form.discipline`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Discipline is a stable field or branch of knowledge whose subject matter, methods, and internal distinctions are coherent enough to support repeated reuse as a concept class.

## Boundary

Included:

- field of knowledge
- stable branch of study
- reusable scope class for subfields
- concept family with recognizable methodological coherence

Excluded:

- a single tool or product area
- a temporary trend or hype label
- an institution or department as such
- a top-level domain so broad that no coherent field boundary remains
