# Information Technology

## Identity

- Canonical label: `information_technology`
- Local id: `form.information_technology`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Information technology is the technology field within computing concerned with the operational deployment, administration, integration, and support of information systems and computing infrastructure.

## Boundary

Included:

- operational computing practice
- systems administration and infrastructure
- deployment and support of information systems
- applied technological management of computing environments

Excluded:

- the whole theoretical scope of computer science
- abstract algorithm study without operational context
- every business process involving information
- electronics outside an information-system role
