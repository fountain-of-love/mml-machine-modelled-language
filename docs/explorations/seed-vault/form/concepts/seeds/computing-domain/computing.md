# Computing

## Identity

- Canonical label: `computing`
- Local id: `form.computing`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Computing is the broad domain concerned with the representation, processing, transmission, storage, and operational use of information through computational systems.

## Boundary

Included:

- computational systems and practices
- information processing
- software and hardware coordination
- umbrella scope for computer science, information technology, and nearby branches

Excluded:

- computer science as the whole of computing
- information technology as the whole of computing
- electronics in every sense regardless of computational role
- any use of calculation language with no system or information-processing context

## Aliases

- information computing
- computational domain
- IT and computing umbrella
