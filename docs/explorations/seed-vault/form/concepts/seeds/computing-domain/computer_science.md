# Computer Science

## Identity

- Canonical label: `computer_science`
- Local id: `form.computer_science`
- Status: `candidate`

## Definition

Computer science is the discipline within computing that studies computation, algorithms, representations, and computational systems in formal, theoretical, and applied forms.

## Boundary

Included:

- algorithms and data structures
- theory of computation
- programming language and systems study
- formal and applied study of computational processes

Excluded:

- all operational IT practice by default
- any software delivery work regardless of disciplinary content
- hardware engineering as such
- information systems governance unless computation remains central

- computational science of computers
