### Matched-resolution completeness test

A model record is structurally auditable when it declares, where applicable:

1. storage/state variables and energy or availability functional;
2. conservation and balance equations;
3. constitutive closures;
4. interconnection and spatial evolution operators;
5. dissipation and entropy-production conditions;
6. initial and boundary conditions;

“Complete” means complete for a declared question, scale, regime, and tolerance—not that every row is populated. This turns the thirteen-level map into a missing-operator diagnostic without erasing physically meaningful asymmetries.

## Evidence Intake 005 — One-Formula Archetype Audit

The proposed scalar template

$$
M\psi_{tt}+R\psi_t+\mathcal K(\psi)
=\nabla\cdot(K\nabla\psi)+N(\psi,\nabla\psi)+s
$$

does **not** yet prove a universal grammar. Unless the admissible forms of $\mathcal K$, $K$, and $N$ are fixed independently, $N$ can absorb an arbitrary residual. The template also does not natively encode algebraic constraints such as incompressibility or Gauss laws, curl-based first-order Maxwell structure, multiple coupled conservation variables, memory, stochastic forcing, or boundary-port power. Levels 11–13 are principally properties or analyses of a model—linearity, stability, and regime classification—not additive terms that one PDE automatically contains.

The programme's one candidate formula remains

$$
\boxed{\mathcal K_r=\kappa\Sigma(\Psi G_r)^2.}
$$

The constrained parent below is not a second candidate law. It is the admissibility framework from which storage, gain, loss, and boundary conditions must be derived:

$$
\dot x=(J-R)\,\delta H+B u,
\qquad
y=B^*\delta H,
$$

with $J^*=-J$, $R^*=R\succeq0$, physically derived $H$, declared operator domains, and an exact boundary-aware balance

$$
\dot H=\langle u,y\rangle-
\langle\delta H,R\delta H\rangle.
$$

This is a structural modelling framework rather than the proposed universal capacity formula. It constrains Resonant Capacity bindings through power preservation, passivity, dimensional typing, state sufficiency, and boundary closure. Finite-dimensional gradients become functional derivatives in field models. Constraints may require descriptor or Dirac structures; irreversible thermodynamics may require an exergetic or explicit energy–entropy/GENERIC extension.

### Proof obligations for a universal grammar theorem

Let $\mathfrak C$ be a frozen corpus of candidate capacity bindings and $\mathfrak A_{RC}$ the admissible Resonant Capacity class. A representation theorem requires, for every $m\in\mathfrak C$:

1. a declared state and boundary map $T_m$;
2. a physically derived $H_m$, not a fitted Lyapunov surrogate;
3. derived $J_m$, $R_m$, and $B_m$ satisfying the structural identities;
4. dimensional and power-port closure;
5. recovery of the original governing equations under $T_m$ without an unrestricted residual function;
6. a stated inverse or equivalence criterion showing what information the representation preserves;
7. derivation of $\Sigma$, $\Psi$, $G_r$, $\kappa$, and $\mathcal K_r$ without redefining their roles;
8. at least one non-member or failure case demonstrating that $\mathfrak A_{RC}$ is not vacuous.

The theorem's conclusion must be written as

$$
\forall m\in\mathfrak C,\quad T_m(m)\in\mathfrak A_{RC},
$$

not as “all physical equations.” The theorem concerns capacity relations satisfying the Resonant Capacity roles and regime, while other equations supply the dynamics, constitutive closure, boundaries, and failure conditions needed to derive or reject a binding. Mechanics, acoustics, electromagnetics, and hydraulic resonators are plausible members. Ordinary passive Fourier thermodynamics is a decisive negative or asymmetric case unless a defensible thermal resonant mode is independently established.

### Required failure controls

The audit must attempt and report mappings expected to fail or require extension:

- an active element with net internal energy supply under the passive sign convention;
- hysteresis represented without its necessary internal memory state;
- a delay or hereditary constitutive law forced into a memoryless $R(x)$;
- incompressible flow represented without its constraint or pressure multiplier;
- Maxwell dynamics represented by a scalar Laplacian while omitting divergence constraints and boundary traces;
- Fourier thermal dynamics treated as a two-store passive resonator;
- a nonlinear but stable system incorrectly classified as unstable.

Passing these negative controls would show that the archetype rejects or lifts inadmissible mappings instead of absorbing them.

### C13 — Circularity and ontology-lock protocol

The engine will use three non-overlapping datasets:

1. **construction set** — examples used to define the slots and primitives;
2. **calibration set** — examples used to refine record mechanics and tolerances without changing the candidate-law meanings;
3. **held-out set** — examples coded only after the ontology, predicates, and rejection rules are frozen.

Any change to a slot definition, primitive, admission predicate, or Resonant Capacity membership rule creates a new engine version. Results obtained under an earlier version are not silently pooled with the revised engine; held-out records affected by the change return to calibration status.

At least two independent codings are required for pivotal held-out records. Disagreement is reported by field and resolved through an explicit adjudication note. Agreement on an under-specified record does not count as validation; both coders must cite the same operational definitions and primary evidence.

### Validation pipeline

```text
source acquisition

→ variable typing
→ dimensional check
→ equation-type classification
→ boundary/regime reconstruction
→ energetic closure
→ matched-resolution decision
→ Resonant Capacity relevance code
→ membership or exclusion certificate
→ independence-tier assignment
→ held-out comparison
```

No imputation occurs before the extraction and admission stages are complete. An imputed record is quarantined from the established matrix until a derivation or experiment supplies provenance and a falsifier.

### Resonant Capacity membership certificate

Every claimed child formula must provide:

1. the capacity functional and measurement convention;
2. a derivation of $\Sigma$ and $\kappa$;
3. the activation coordinate $\Psi$ and its units;
4. the source–response transfer function defining $G_r$;
5. proof that $G_r$ amplifies the same coordinate appearing in the quadratic capacity;
6. an independently fixed baseline and parameter intervention;
7. evidence that a resonance, rather than relaxation or generic amplification, exists;
8. boundary, topology, and validity conditions;
9. an energy/entropy/exergy audit where applicable;
10. a falsification or non-membership condition.

Failure at items 1–6 makes the binding invalid. Failure at item 7 classifies it outside the resonant member even if a quadratic capacity exists. Items 8–10 determine the certified scope.

### Engine evaluation outputs

The engine reports four different quantities rather than one composite score:

- **coverage:** which prespecified records received an admissible classification;
- **structural validity:** which records pass the admission predicates;
- **Resonant Capacity support:** which records receive a membership certificate;
- **predictive increment:** performance beyond the domain-standard null model on held-out cases.

These quantities must not be collapsed into a weighted “universality score” unless the weights are externally justified. Keeping them separate prevents excellent documentation coverage from compensating for absent predictive evidence.

## Evidence Intake 008 — Two-View Architecture

The engine will expose two synchronized but non-equivalent views:

```text
Scientific view: 13 qualified operator/analysis slots
Navigation view: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 gate projection
```

The scientific view is authoritative for classification, derivation, and evidence. The gate view is a lossy projection used for exposition and research navigation. It cannot change a record's equation type, maturity, admissibility, Resonant Capacity relevance, or evidence tier.

### Projection contract

Every gate assignment must specify:

- which scientific slots it contains or highlights;
- whether the relationship is inclusion, thematic association, or sequence;
- what information is hidden by the projection;
- whether another reasonable grouping gives the same scientific conclusions;
- whether any quantitative prediction depends on the Fibonacci number itself.

Unless the final item is supported prospectively, the gate number has no evidential weight.

### Gap-generation predicate

The engine may open an imputation question only when

$$
\operatorname{GapCandidate}(r)
=
\operatorname{RoleExpected}(r)
\land\operatorname{EvidenceAbsent}(r)
\land\neg\operatorname{PhysicallyExcluded}(r)
\land\operatorname{Testable}(r).
$$

`RoleExpected` requires a derivation from a conservation law, state requirement, constitutive closure, reduction theorem, symmetry, or observed unexplained residual. It cannot be satisfied merely because another domain has a visually attractive formula in the same row.

### Analogy-to-imputation escalation

An analogue becomes an imputation candidate only after it passes:

1. role correspondence;
2. dimensional typing;
3. causal/mechanistic correspondence;
4. compatible abstraction and regime;
5. a nontrivial predicted consequence;
6. an explicit non-analogue or rejection case.

This rule is especially important for buoyancy, pressure, inertia, and threshold analogies, where shared words can hide different physical structures.

### Matrix display rule

The rendered matrix may show compact badges, but the underlying record keeps separate fields for relation type, epistemic status, mapping verdict, applicability, and evidence role. `[N]` means no meaningful direct analogue at the declared resolution; it is not a failure of nature or an invitation to invent one.

### C14 — Incremental-value protocol for the Fibonacci scaffold

The primary ontology is a typed graph. The displayed 1–13 sequence is a readable traversal, not proof that the graph is a path. Fibonacci gates define a projection over that graph.

Before evaluation, freeze gate definitions and assignments, assignment meanings, evaluation tasks, metrics and minimum useful effects, alternative groupings, permutation controls, and treatment of ambiguous or multiple assignments.

Use the same held-out records under at least four conditions:

1. qualified thirteen-slot engine without gates;
2. Fibonacci-gated view;
3. conventional grouping by equation or energy-system role;
4. permuted labels with matched group sizes.

Where feasible, blind coders to the preferred condition. Report accuracy, error type, completion time, agreement, abstention, and adjudication separately rather than constructing a score after observing results.

### Masked-record imputation experiment

For each held-out trial, block source leakage, hide one known relation or qualification, expose only prespecified neighbours and metadata, require a prediction of role and constraint class before symbolic detail, permit `absent/inapplicable`, and lock the prediction before revealing the source. Score role, dimensions, operator order, boundary/regime requirements, and rejection decision.

Predicting “some coefficient” or “some nonlinear term” is insufficient unless that resolution was accepted in advance. Evidence already exposed elsewhere in the prompt, model context, or accessible corpus is not genuinely held out.

### Decision rule

- No incremental performance: retain Fibonacci as optional exposition.
- Improvement on a prespecified task: claim organizational utility for that task only.
- Successful numerical Fibonacci-dependent prediction: open a separate physical-evidence protocol.

None of these outcomes changes Resonant Capacity maturity automatically. The scaffold and candidate law keep separate evidence ledgers.

## Evidence Intake 009 — Layered Publication Architecture

The project will maintain three synchronized representations:

| Layer | Audience and purpose | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic-law façade | Reader orientation through familiar laws and plain-language questions | Expository only |
| Thirteen-slot academic engine | Qualified formulas, provenance, drift, applicability, and imputation governance | Authoritative record layer |
| Resonant Capacity proof view | Membership certificates, independence tiers, null comparisons, and held-out predictions | Authoritative claim layer |

Every façade cell contains references to canonical engine records. A cell may point to multiple records when the simple wording compresses distinct balance, constitutive, boundary, or dynamical relations. If no valid record supports it, the façade marks it as analogy, open question, or non-applicable rather than displaying a formula as established.

### Translation contract

For each façade statement, record:

1. the canonical record ID or IDs;
2. which details were suppressed for accessibility;
3. the exact validity sentence that must remain visible;
4. whether the example is a law, constitutive relation, reduced model, solution, or phenomenon;
5. whether it has any Resonant Capacity relevance;
6. the condition under which the simplified wording becomes misleading.

This allows a school-level phrase such as “flow meets resistance” to remain useful while distinguishing a pure resistor from a general impedance, nonlinear drag, memory, or frequency-dependent loss.

### Pedagogical validation

The façade should be tested for comprehension without testing it as physics evidence. Candidate outcomes include whether readers correctly identify storage versus transfer, resistance versus impedance, relaxation versus resonance, and analogy versus derived equivalence after using the table. Increased confidence accompanied by increased classification error is a failed pedagogical result.

### Gap-question routing

Plain-language questions such as “what stores energy?” or “what happens at the boundary?” route to the academic engine. They do not generate gaps directly. The engine first returns applicability, existing terminology, physical exclusion, or unresolved status; only the previously defined `GapCandidate` predicate can open imputation.

## Evidence Intake 012 — Operator-Completeness and Drift Corpus

The new discovery corpus supplies candidate records across thirteen recurring model questions: balance, storage, gradient, constitutive response, flux, equilibrium, propagation, resonance, dissipation, boundary, superposition, nonlinearity, and regime scaling. It is ingested as a hypothesis-discovery dataset, not as a prequalified formula matrix.

### Normalization rules

Before a row enters the authoritative engine:

1. split governing laws, constitutive closures, reduced models, particular solutions, and dimensionless diagnostics into separate records;
2. replace broad “universal formula” wording with the exact operator role and declared assumptions;
3. distinguish resistance from complex impedance and relaxation from resonance;
4. attach local or integral balance and boundary terms where conservation is claimed;
5. derive characteristic scales before accepting a dimensionless group;
6. keep nonphysical metaphors outside the five-domain evidence corpus;
7. record `absent/degenerate` when the physics does not contain the requested role.

### Drift pipeline

```text
observed model
  -> missing-role screen
  -> search existing qualified relations
  -> test physical applicability
  -> derive candidate operator and dimensions
  -> predict a held-out consequence
  -> accept, restrict, or reject
```

The engine must distinguish omission from legitimate reduction. For example, an absent inertive term in Fourier heat conduction is a meaningful diffusive limit, whereas omission of damping from a finite-$Q$ resonator is an incomplete model. Likewise, lack of superposition in a nonlinear regime is not a missing law.

### Highest-value prospective tests

- predict a boundary leakage or reflection term before measuring the held-out interface;
- predict a loss operator required to reconcile gain and energy balance;
- derive a dimensionless boundary for linear Resonant Capacity validity;
- predict which additional state is required when a diffusive model becomes wave-like;
- predict the onset at which resonance gain saturates or becomes unstable.

These are candidate R4 pathways only when locked before observation and shown to add information beyond the standard child model. Retrospective identification remains discovery evidence.
