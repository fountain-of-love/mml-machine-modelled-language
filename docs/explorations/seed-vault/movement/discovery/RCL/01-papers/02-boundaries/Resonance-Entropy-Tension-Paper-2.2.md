## Cross-Cutting Regime Condition

Near equilibrium, resonance may remain linear and entropy production approximately quadratic. Far from equilibrium, amplification may saturate, coefficients may become state-dependent, and new dissipative regimes may emerge.

## Evidence Intake 001 — Resonance, Coherence, and Dissipation

Resonance is restricted to a declared transfer function, natural frequency, damping, input, and output. For a conventional linear second-order response, detuning $r=\omega/\omega_0$ and quality factor $Q$ may enter a normalized gain, but the numerator depends on the observed variable. Broader coherence through phase, geometry, timing, or coupling must not be silently represented by the same $G_r$.

For a periodically driven weak-loss steady state, the conventional quality factor is

$$
Q
=
2\pi\frac{E_{\mathrm{stored}}}{\Delta E_{\mathrm{lost,cycle}}}.
$$

Only when the lost work is thermalized within the chosen environment at temperature $T_0$ may one identify $\Delta E_{\mathrm{lost,cycle}}=T_0\Delta S_{\mathrm{gen,cycle}}$. Radiation or exported energy is a boundary output, not automatically internal entropy generation.

Likewise, $B_{\mathrm{realized}}=B_{\mathrm{activated}}-T_0S_{\mathrm{generated}}$ is only a reduced process balance when supplied or initial exergy, accumulation, useful output, other outputs, boundary, and interval are consistently defined. It is not a general state equation.

**Maturity boundary:** quadratic storage and conventional linear response are established; a domain-neutral identification of resonance with all forms of coherence remains proposed.

## Evidence Intake 002 — Emergent Resonance

Resonance is not a fourth lumped element. It is a response of a coupled storage topology. For the canonical series model,

$$
\omega_0=\frac1{\sqrt{\mathcal I\mathcal C}},
\qquad
Q=\frac1{\mathcal R}\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal I}{\mathcal C}}.
$$

Inertance and compliance permit reversible exchange; resistance removes energy from the selected recoverable Hamiltonian and sets damping. However, the mere presence of two storage coefficients does not guarantee an observable resonant peak: topology, damping ratio, forcing, and output selection matter.

The quality-factor identity

$$
Q=\omega_0\frac{H_{\mathrm{stored}}}{P_{\mathrm{loss}}}
$$

also exposes two different scaling experiments. Fixed source effort in the canonical flow response can yield $H\propto Q^2$ because source power rises as resistance falls. Fixed input power yields $H=(Q/\omega_0)P_{\mathrm{in}}$. These protocols must not be merged.
