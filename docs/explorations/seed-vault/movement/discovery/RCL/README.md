# RCL — Resonant Capacity Law

This folder contains the Resonant Capacity Law work curated under the Seed Vault's `movement/discovery` element. It is an equation-led scientific derivation together with the governed knowledge structure around it. It derives Resonant Capacity as a universal representation law for a declared class of stable quadratic two-store systems, then binds the same parent structure to mechanics, second-sound thermodynamics, acoustics, electromagnetism, and hydraulics without changing its frozen definitions and conventions.

The vault is also a foundation for **Machine Modelled Language (MML)**. Its concepts, semantic roles, equations, constraints, provenance, and failure conditions provide governed scientific structure that a Common Language Model can make addressable. MML can then compile selected discoveries into mathematical weights and task-specific operators, allowing scientific knowledge to participate directly in deterministic mathematical operations.

The present proof is deliberately analytical and equation-led, establishing a clear admitted model class before machine-verifiable proof software or wider empirical work is introduced. Its universality is bounded by that class; experimental replication and stronger empirical claims are explicit extensions of the package.

## Architectural Role

- **CML** is the **Common Language Model** under development: the shared schema defining concepts, relations, evidence, constraints, maturity, and topology.
- **Seed Vault** is this knowledge base: the durable content organized according to CML.
- **MML** supplies the executable semantic mechanism that makes this larger CML-shaped knowledge base addressable and reusable by machines.
- **LLMs** assist discovery and expression, but candidate knowledge must be validated before deposit.

The vault can preserve established knowledge, competing interpretations, negative results, counterexamples, open questions, and imputed candidates without presenting them as equally mature.
