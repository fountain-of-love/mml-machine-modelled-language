### Programme Decision Gate

After C01–C16 have been resolved, bounded, or retained as explicit negative results, the paper series will evaluate three possible outcomes:

1. **Derived metamodel theorem:** the relation is proven for every system satisfying an explicitly declared canonical storage, topology, linearity, and excitation contract. This is rigorous synthesis, but not a new empirical law.
2. **Broader law-level claim:** justified only if fixed operational bindings produce nontrivial out-of-sample predictions across genuinely distinct realizations and outperform the declared null model.
3. **Hypothesis-generating comparative grammar:** preferred if the demonstrated value is explanatory compression, disciplined transposition, gap detection, and informative failure rather than new predictive invariance.

The second outcome is not a failed version of the first. It is a distinct and potentially publishable methodological contribution.

### Robustness Audit of Intake 002

The expanded canonical expression

$$
H_{\mathcal I,r}
=\frac{\mathcal I^2}{2\mathcal C\mathcal R^2}f_0^2
$$

is conditional on treating $f_0$ as the controlled baseline. In the fixed source-effort experiment used to obtain $G_f=Q$,

$$
f_0=\frac{e_0}{\omega_0\mathcal I}
$$

depends on $\mathcal I$ and $\mathcal C$. Substitution gives

$$
H_{\mathcal I,r}=\frac{\mathcal I e_0^2}{2\mathcal R^2}.
$$

Therefore no causal parameter ranking may be inferred from the expanded formula until the intervention—fixed $f_0$, fixed $e_0$, fixed $P_{\mathrm{in}}$, fixed $\omega_0$, or another constraint—is declared. Likewise $H=(Q/\omega_0)P_{\mathrm{in}}$ is linear in $Q$ only when $P_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $\omega_0$ are held fixed.

### Evidence Intake 005 — Constrained One-Formula Archetype Audit

**Status:** the scalar universal-field template is rejected as presently underconstrained. The Resonant Capacity Law remains the sole candidate universal formula; typed open dissipative Hamiltonian structure is retained only as its admissibility framework.

The attachment was identical to Evidence Intake 004, but the new evaluation question changes its use. The expression containing unrestricted $\mathcal K(\psi)$ and $N(\psi,\nabla\psi)$ can absorb arbitrary residual physics and therefore cannot replace or prove the candidate law. The programme will test Resonant Capacity bindings using the constrained structure

$$
\dot x=(J-R)\delta H+Bu,
\qquad
y=B^*\delta H,
\qquad
J^*=-J,quad R^*=R\succeq0,
$$

including its boundary-aware energy inequality as an audit framework. Membership in the candidate law requires a physically derived capacity and substrate coefficient, a correctly typed activation, an independently defined resonant gain, dimensional closure, state sufficiency, and recovery of the capacity relation without free residual functions. Thermodynamic members must additionally preserve energy conservation and non-negative entropy production through an exergetic or energy–entropy extension.

The required outcome is now two-layered:

## Shared Paper Anatomy

Every short note should add one primary contribution and use the same anatomy:

1. **Proposition** — one sentence stating the claim.
2. **Why it matters** — the problem in two or three sentences.
3. **Core formula** — one principal equation.
4. **Cross-domain transposal** — a compact comparison table.
5. **Drift detector** — one question revealing incompleteness.
6. **Imputation opportunity** — one candidate missing variable, coefficient, or operator.
7. **Falsifiable implication** — a measurable test.
8. **Spiral link** — what the paper inherits and what the next paper adds.

Every principal relation should also instantiate the qualified-relation record introduced by Paper 0: equation, physical role, domain, variable binding, units, sign convention, abstraction level, validity regime, boundary, maturity, and provenance. For response relations, add source, input, output, baseline, observation point, and transient or steady-state convention. For thermodynamic relations, add environment and entropy-production treatment.

Each header should state series title, spiral stage, paper number, and claim maturity: **established**, **synthesis**, **hypothesis**, or **speculation**. A one-page note should not attempt a full literature review; references may occupy a second page or shared bibliography.
