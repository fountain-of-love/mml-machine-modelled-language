## Assigned Scientific Concerns

### C01 — Identity versus independent derivation

Separate four claim types:

1. a kinematic identity: a quadratic measure scales with the square of an amplitude ratio;
2. a constitutive binding: a specified substrate coefficient defines stored energy;
3. a dynamical model: forcing, damping, frequency, and boundary conditions determine the response through a closed system of equations;
4. a cross-domain hypothesis: normalized results share content not already guaranteed by linear modal theory.

The principle earns **derived status** when $G_r$ and the target capacity follow from independently specified governing, constitutive, source, and boundary equations without inserting the measured target amplitude. This is sufficient for the representation-theorem claim and closes C11 at that level. Predictive novelty beyond the child equations is a separate optional R4 question.

### C03 — Governance of $\kappa$

For every binding, derive and freeze $\kappa$ before comparison. Record its units, convention, and dependencies. Reject any binding in which $\kappa$ acts as a free fit or absorbs missing geometry, frequency, damping, material, source, load, or boundary physics. Prefer a nondimensional invariant where possible.

#### C03 resolution proposal adopted in R3-v1.0

$\kappa$ is removed from the domain fit space. It is globally fixed to $1/2$ by the peak-flow-amplitude and total-cycle-mean-energy-at-resonance conventions. Storage geometry and modal normalization belong in $\Sigma$; coupling belongs in explicit input/output operators; damping, loading, and leakage belong in $\mathcal R$ and $G_r$; frequency belongs in the transfer function; and nonlinear or regime effects trigger failure or a versioned lift.

The five bindings all reduce symbolically to $\mathcal K_r=\tfrac12\mathcal I f_r^2$ under the same convention. The apparatus-specific uncertainty in second-sound $M_2$ affects $\Sigma$, not $\kappa$. A certificate that needs to fit $\kappa$ is rejected. C03 is **closed for R3-v1.0 analytical governance**; empirical discrepancies remain diagnostic tests of the child model rather than occasions to reopen the coefficient.

### C09 — Protocol dependence of gain

Define

$$
G_r(\omega)
=
\left|\frac{Y(\omega)}{U(\omega)}\right|
\bigg/
G_{\mathrm{baseline}},
$$

with domain-specific input $U$, output $Y$, baseline, units or normalization, source and load impedances, observation point, and transient or steady-state convention. “Amplitude-controlled” must specify whether it refers to the source or the response.

#### C09 resolution

The cross-domain analogue inventory determines which contextual roles must be bound; R3-v1.0 then freezes their operational values. Every admitted certificate uses fixed sinusoidal source effort at the one-port, common series flow as output, steady-state peak response at $\omega_0$, baseline $f_0=e_0/(\omega_0\mathcal I)$, $G_r=Q$, total cycle-mean modal energy, and source/load effects included in or explicitly connected to the total $\mathcal I$, $\mathcal C$, and $\mathcal R$. The per-domain bindings are recorded in the certificate dossier's C09 matrix.

C09 is therefore **closed for R3-v1.0**. Alternative contexts are permitted only as versioned protocols with their own complete bindings; they do not make the frozen result ambiguous.

### C11 — Derivational content beyond resemblance

The governing concern was:

> Whether the established canonical-model result supports a broader law depends on additional predictive content across genuinely distinct physical realizations.

The frozen systems-of-equations proof resolves that concern for the representation-theorem claim: the parent archetype regenerates the admitted child systems without primitive redefinition and records failed admission. The stronger question of a novel empirical invariant beyond the represented class is retained as optional R4 work.

### C16 — Strong relativistic resonance hypothesis

H-RR is admitted as a separate high-risk branch: $c=\omega_C\bar\lambda_C$ may define a generalized rate amplitude of a Lorentz-covariant rest-frame periodic coordinate, and the complete internal storage may yield $mc^2$. Governance is stricter than algebraic substitution. Mass, amplitude, frequency, storage metric, and topology must be independently derived; energy components must be non-overlapping; relativistic dispersion must be recovered; and at least one consequence must exceed the identities $E=mc^2$, $E=\hbar\omega$, and $c=\omega_C\bar\lambda_C$. No missing factor may be assigned to $\kappa$.

Until that contract is met, H-RR has hypothesis status and contributes no R3/R4 evidence. This keeps the possibility inside the programme without allowing it to validate itself through a change of notation.

This does not make exhaustive pattern detection scientifically trivial. Because the prespecified child equations are derived from one restrictive, role-preserving archetype under the matched-resolution contract, the result is a universal **representation theorem over that declared class**. This closes C11 for Paper 0's theorem and comparative-grammar claims. Whether the theorem also yields a new empirical invariant beyond classifying and regenerating its members is a separate optional R4 question. The achievements must remain distinct: “not yet a new empirical law” does not mean “not a proved universal grammar.”

C11 closure is based on:

1. one frozen parent system and storage functional;
2. fixed parameters, source protocol, baseline, gain, and normalization;
3. recovery of each admitted child by role-preserving substitution;
4. dimensional and power closure;
5. negative controls predicting absence, operator change, or breakdown;
6. no unrestricted functions, target-amplitude inputs, or child-specific primitive changes.

### Law-or-grammar decision test

After the domain papers report, classify the outcome:

- **candidate law** only if fixed operational definitions yield nontrivial, cross-domain predictions and survive negative controls;
- **comparative grammar** if the robust contribution is qualification, transposition, explanatory compression, and failure detection;
- **rejected synthesis** if neither predictive gain nor comparative utility is demonstrated.

**Resolution evidence:** preregistered rejection conditions, fixed parameters, independent gain estimates, domain results including failures, and an explicit final maturity decision.

## Evidence Intake 001 — Nested Claims and Independent Regime Axes

The supplied material supports the following nested claim structure:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{quadratic family:}\quad &\mathcal K=\kappa S\Psi^2,\\
\text{modal member:}\quad &\mathcal K=\kappa S(\omega A)^2,\\
\text{resonant member:}\quad &\mathcal K_r=\kappa S(\omega A G_r)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

These levels must not inherit one another's maturity. The quadratic family is a classification; the modal expression is established for suitable harmonic storage coordinates; the resonant expression becomes predictive only when $G_r$ is independently determined.

Near equilibrium and near resonance are separate axes:

| | Off resonance | Near resonance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Near equilibrium** | small linear response | potentially amplified but still linear response |
| **Far from equilibrium** | nonlinear response without modal matching | amplification with saturation, instability, or regime change likely |

For a conventional second-order linear mode, a candidate response form is

$$
G(r)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{(1-r^2)^2+(r/Q)^2}},
\qquad r=\omega/\omega_0,
$$

but the numerator and normalization depend on the chosen input and output. Therefore 432 Hz and 440 Hz have no universal hierarchy: only detuning relative to a declared $\omega_0$, quality factor, geometry, material, boundary, and source protocol has physical meaning.

The proposed realized-capacity bound

$$
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{realized}}
\le
\min(
\mathcal K_r,
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{source}},
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{material}},
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{boundary}},
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{nonlinear}}
)
$$

is retained as a governance placeholder, not yet a literal theorem: the listed limits must first be defined on compatible quantities, time horizons, and feasible-state sets.

## Evidence Intake 002 — Excitation-Condition Scaling

For the canonical series resonator driven by fixed source-effort amplitude $e_0$, with flow measured at $\omega_0$ and baseline $f_0=e_0/(\omega_0\mathcal I)$,

$$
G_f=Q,
\qquad
H_r=\frac12\mathcal I(f_0Q)^2.
$$

The $Q^2$ scaling is therefore exact for this declared comparison. It is not a generic consequence of the phrase “fixed amplitude.” The fixed quantity, response variable, reference amplitude, topology, and parameter variation must all be stated.

At periodic steady state with fixed average input power and no other net energy outlet,

$$
P_{\mathrm{in}}=P_{\mathrm{loss}},
\qquad
Q=\omega_0\frac{H_{\mathrm{stored}}}{P_{\mathrm{loss}}},
$$

so

$$
H_{\mathrm{stored}}
=\frac{Q}{\omega_0}P_{\mathrm{in}}.
$$

Stored energy is then linear in $Q$. Fixed maximum response instead yields a substrate and allowable-amplitude limit, while nonlinear or saturated systems generally follow neither scaling globally.

The phrase “linear in $Q$” assumes fixed $P_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $\omega_0$. If parameter changes also move $\omega_0$, the controlled intervention must be stated and the full $Q/\omega_0$ dependence retained.

This becomes a mandatory governance field:

| Field | Required declaration |
| --- | --- |
| Controlled quantity | source effort, source flow, source power, or response limit |
| Response | effort, flow, state coordinate, field, or total stored energy |
| Baseline | explicit off-resonant, quasi-static, inertive, compliant, or matched reference |
| Parameter variation | what changes when $Q$, $\mathcal I$, $\mathcal C$, or $\mathcal R$ changes |
| Energy convention | instantaneous, peak, cycle-mean, or total modal energy |

The “derived law of activation” interpretation is accepted at the canonical-model level. Any optional claim of new empirical law-level novelty would require a result not already exhausted by the transfer function and quadratic Hamiltonian.

## Optional R4 Empirical-Law Validation Protocol

Three claim levels will be tested separately:

| Claim | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Canonical theorem** | Derivation from declared linear storage, interconnection, dissipation, topology, and excitation assumptions |
| **Cross-realization invariant** | Parameter-free or prespecified dimensionless relation holding out of sample across Tier B realizations |
| **Broader law** | A successful prediction, bound, correction, or failure boundary not already entailed by each realization's standard model |

A normalized identity such as

$$
\frac{H_r}{\kappa S A_0^2}=|G_r|^2
$$

cannot establish an R4 empirical increment if $G_r$ is measured from the same response used to calculate $H_r$. It may validate bookkeeping, but it contains no independent predictive information.

Candidate optional R4 tests include:

- predict a held-out capacity using independently measured constitutive parameters and source conditions, with no post hoc $\kappa$ or gain fit;
- predict which apparent domain cell must be structurally absent;
- predict the operator or extra state required when the lumped grammar fails;
- predict a dimensionless bound or correction shared by distinct constitutive realizations;
- predict the onset of a failure regime before observing the held-out response.

Evidence Intake 004 adds a particularly useful candidate: use the qualified operator map to predict which operator or state must be restored when a held-out reduced model crosses its declared scale or regime boundary. The prediction must specify the trigger, expected correction or qualitative failure, and tolerance before the target response is inspected. A post hoc statement that “nonlinearity,” “boundaries,” or “dissipation” mattered does not exceed the null model.

## Evidence Intake 008 — Gate Labels Carry No Law Weight

Fibonacci gate assignment is metadata with Resonant Capacity relevance code **0** unless it independently fixes or predicts $\mathcal K_r$, $\Sigma$, $\Psi$, $G_r$, $\kappa$, a qualification, or an exclusion before the target case is observed. A binding cannot gain maturity because it occupies gate 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, or 13.

Likewise, an empty gate/domain cell is not evidence for a missing law. The engine may propose imputation only when a physical derivation predicts the missing role and supplies a falsifiable consequence. This blocks “analogue hunting,” in which phenomena such as levitation, radiation pressure, or thermal expansion are renamed as buoyancy without preserving the Archimedean mechanism.
