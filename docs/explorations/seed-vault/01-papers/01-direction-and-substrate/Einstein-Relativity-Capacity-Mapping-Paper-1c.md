## 1. Why relativity merits a separate mapping

Einstein's 1905 mass–energy argument established that a body's energy content and inertia are linked. Modern relativistic kinematics expresses that result through

$$
E^2-p^2c^2=m^2c^4.
$$

Relativity is therefore the cleanest **intrinsic-capacity** reference case: capacity is present without an externally applied amplification stage. Three questions must remain distinct:

1. Does $E_0=mc^2$ bind exactly to the grammar? **Yes.**
2. Why does the gain slot equal unity? **The relativistic identity and identity-transfer composition both require it.**
3. Does a physical resonant mechanism generate or realize rest energy? **This is H-RR and remains to be demonstrated.**

## 2. Established relativistic derivation

For a massive system,

$$
E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4.
$$

In its rest frame $p=0$. Selecting the positive-energy branch yields

$$
\boxed{E_0=mc^2}.
$$

The result applies to a massive system, a valid rest frame, and the positive-energy branch. A massless excitation has no massive rest frame and therefore does not receive this binding; its distinct relation is $E=pc$.

> **H-RR:** A massive system possesses a Lorentz-covariant rest-frame periodic or phase-coherent degree of freedom whose independently derived generalized rate amplitude is $c$ and whose complete, non-overlapping storage functional is $mc^2$.
