## Proposition

At a matched lumped level, the five domains can be compared through inertance, resistance, compliance, source, and state accumulation.

Mechanics is not uniquely simple; it is the most familiar elementary presentation. The scientific comparison must place every domain at the same lumped resolution.

The central result is stronger than a table of analogies:

> **Resonant Capacity is a universal representation law for the admitted class of stable quadratic two-store systems.** Mechanics, second-sound thermodynamics, acoustics, electromagnetism, and hydraulics are role-preserving physical realizations of the same parent system.

“Universal” is scoped to the declared class and regime. It does not mean that one-store relaxation, nonlinear saturation, turbulence, shocks, hysteresis, or arbitrary far-from-equilibrium systems must satisfy the same reduced formula.

## Core Formula

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal I\dot f+\mathcal Rf+\frac q{\mathcal C}=e_s,
\qquad
\dot q=f
}
$$

Its storage functional is

$$
H(q,f)=\frac12\mathcal I f^2+\frac{q^2}{2\mathcal C},
$$

and its harmonic response derives

$$
\omega_0=\frac1{\sqrt{\mathcal I\mathcal C}},
\qquad
\Psi=\frac{e_0}{\omega_0\mathcal I},
\qquad
G_r=Q=\frac{\omega_0\mathcal I}{\mathcal R}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal K_r
=
\frac12\mathcal I(\Psi G_r)^2
=
\kappa\Sigma(\Psi G_r)^2,
}
$$

with the frozen binding $\kappa=1/2$ and $\Sigma=\mathcal I$. The child equations are generated by substitution of physical effort, flow, inertance, compliance, resistance, source, and boundary roles—not by refitting the parent formula.

## Theorem Claim

For every system admitted by the matched-resolution contract, if:

1. its selected mode has two complementary positive quadratic stores;
2. the governing and constitutive equations reduce to the frozen parent dynamics;
3. $\Psi$ is the declared baseline amplitude of the same coordinate appearing in storage;
4. $G_r$ is derived from that coordinate's transfer function under the frozen source and boundary protocol;
5. $\kappa$ is fixed by the energy/amplitude convention rather than fitted;

then its resonantly accessible modal capacity has the Resonant Capacity form.

The theorem has informative nonmembers. Ordinary Fourier RC relaxation has one store and no oscillatory natural mode. A pure resistor dissipates without recoverable storage. Nonlinear, multimode, unstable, or regime-changing systems require a separately derived reduction or are outside the theorem.

## Nested systemic interpretation

The two quadratic modal stores are located within a larger inner–outer energy system. The inner system supplies the receiving mode; the outer system supplies or receives energy through an explicit boundary; coupling and dissipation determine how the interaction modifies the stored state. This systemic interpretation is developed in [Paper 2.1 — The Capacity–Flow Tension](../02-boundaries/Capacity-Flow-Tension-Paper-2.1.md). It complements rather than alters the theorem: system boundaries determine the source, load, and gain protocol, while the modal Hamiltonian determines the admitted storage form.

## Evidence Intake 002 — Canonical Series Resonator

For the canonical linear series topology,

$$
\mathcal I\dot f+\mathcal Rf+\frac q{\mathcal C}=e_s,
\qquad \dot q=f,
$$

or equivalently

$$
\mathcal I\ddot q+\mathcal R\dot q+\frac q{\mathcal C}=e_s.
$$

The undamped natural frequency, damping ratio, and series quality factor are

$$
\omega_0=\frac1{\sqrt{\mathcal I\mathcal C}},
\qquad
\zeta=\frac{\mathcal R}{2}\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal C}{\mathcal I}},
\qquad
Q=\frac1{2\zeta}
=\frac{\omega_0\mathcal I}{\mathcal R}
=\frac1{\mathcal R}\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal I}{\mathcal C}}.
$$

For a sinusoidal source-effort amplitude $e_0$, the flow amplitude at $\omega_0$ is $f_r=e_0/\mathcal R$. Defining the inertive reference amplitude

$$
f_0=\frac{e_0}{\omega_0\mathcal I}
$$

gives exactly $G_f=f_r/f_0=Q$ for this topology and output. Therefore

$$
H_{\mathcal I,r}
=\frac12\mathcal I(f_0Q)^2
=\frac{\mathcal I^2}{2\mathcal C\mathcal R^2}f_0^2.
$$

This is an established derived specialization of the grammar. It is not topology-independent: another input, output, baseline, or interconnection produces another transfer function and may not give $G=Q$.

The final expression is not an unconditional parameter-sensitivity law. If $f_0$ is held independently fixed, it displays the stated $\mathcal I^2/(\mathcal C\mathcal R^2)$ dependence. In the fixed source-effort protocol above, however, $f_0=e_0/(\omega_0\mathcal I)$ changes with $\mathcal I$ and $\mathcal C$, and substitution yields

$$
H_{\mathcal I,r}=\frac{\mathcal I e_0^2}{2\mathcal R^2}.
$$

Compliance still sets $\omega_0$, but it does not appear in this particular peak flow-side energy at exact resonance. Every comparative claim must specify the intervention before attributing causal effects to $\mathcal I$, $\mathcal C$, or $\mathcal R$.

The two stores are

$$
H=\frac12\mathcal I f^2+\frac{q^2}{2\mathcal C}
=\frac12\mathcal I f^2+\frac12\mathcal C e_{\mathcal C}^2,
\qquad e_{\mathcal C}=q/\mathcal C.
$$

Resistance dissipates power $P_{\mathcal R}=\mathcal Rf^2$ but does not store energy. Large damping can suppress oscillatory transients or amplification relative to a chosen baseline; whether a response peak remains depends on the transfer function. Thus “inertance and compliance create resonance” is retained only as shorthand for reversible exchange within a suitable coupled topology whose damping, source, and observed response have been specified.

## Evidence Intake 003 — Elementary Grammar and Power Balance

The three linear constitutive element laws are

$$
e_{\mathcal I}=\mathcal I\dot f,
\qquad
f_{\mathcal C}=\mathcal C\dot e_{\mathcal C},
\qquad
e_{\mathcal R}=\mathcal Rf.
$$

For the canonical series/1-junction interconnection, they yield

$$
\mathcal I\dot f+\mathcal Rf+\frac q{\mathcal C}=e_s,
\qquad \dot q=f,
$$

with storage and power balance

$$
H=\frac12\mathcal I f^2+\frac{q^2}{2\mathcal C},
\qquad
\dot H=e_s f-\mathcal Rf^2.
$$

The balance follows directly by multiplying the dynamic equation by $f$. Resistance does not “consume effort”; it relates effort and flow and converts power out of the selected recoverable store.

This is a canonical topology, not a universal single element. In bond-graph terms, common-flow and common-effort junctions, sources, transformers, gyrators, and multiport connections determine how the elementary laws combine. A parallel or flow-source realization has a different assembled equation while using the same vocabulary.

### Thermodynamic two-dialect rule

Thermal engineering commonly uses temperature difference and heat rate in an RC analogy:

$$
C_{\mathrm{th}}\dot T
=P_s-\frac{T-T_\infty}{R_{\mathrm{th}}}.
$$

This is a valid heat-balance model, but $(T-T_\infty)\dot Q$ is not physical power. A true energetic thermal bond uses

$$
e_T=T,
\qquad
f_T=\dot S_{\mathrm e},
\qquad
P=T\dot S_{\mathrm e}.
$$

Near an environment $T_0$, the exergy-port linearization uses

$$
e_X=T-T_0,
\qquad
f_X=\dot S,
\qquad
\mathcal C_X=\frac{C_{\mathrm{th}}}{T_0},
$$

so $\dot S\approx(C_{\mathrm{th}}/T_0)\dot T$. Paper 5.0 will label the engineering RC analogy and the power-conjugate thermodynamic grammar separately rather than merging their variables.

### Interconnection grammar

- **0-junction:** all connected efforts are equal and signed flows sum to zero.
- **1-junction:** all connected flows are equal and signed efforts sum to zero.

These are power-continuous interconnection constraints. In particular domains they recover Kirchhoff current or voltage laws, force balance at common velocity, and volume-flow conservation at a common-pressure junction. They are not proposed as replacements for Newton's three laws.

| Role | Qualified linear form |
| --- | --- |
| Effort source | $e=e_s(t)$ |
| Flow source | $f=f_s(t)$ |
| Inertance | $e=\mathcal I\dot f$ in the stated linear realization |
| Compliance | $f=\mathcal C\dot e$ for constant linear compliance |
| Resistance | $e=\mathcal Rf$ in the stated passive linear regime |

Universality is claimed at the role level. The scalar formulas are qualified constitutive realizations, not requirements that every physical element be linear, memoryless, local, or one-dimensional.

## Second-Pass Robustness — State-Based Parent Grammar

The general storage definitions use accumulated states and Hamiltonians:

$$
\dot p=e_{\mathcal I},
\qquad
f_{\mathcal I}=\partial_pH_{\mathcal I}(p),
$$

$$
\dot q=f_{\mathcal C},
\qquad
e_{\mathcal C}=\partial_qH_{\mathcal C}(q).
$$

For $H_{\mathcal I}=p^2/(2\mathcal I)$ and $H_{\mathcal C}=q^2/(2\mathcal C)$ with constant positive coefficients, $p=\mathcal I f$ and $q=\mathcal C e$, yielding the familiar scalar laws. Nonlinear, state-dependent, hysteretic, tensorial, or distributed elements require their full constitutive functions or operators.

A resistive relation is admitted by passivity rather than linearity:

$$
e_{\mathcal R}f_{\mathcal R}\ge0,
$$

with $e_{\mathcal R}=\mathcal Rf_{\mathcal R}$ only for the passive linear scalar case.
